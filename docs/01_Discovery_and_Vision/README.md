# Discovery & Vision

## Purpose

The Discovery & Vision phase establishes the business problem, validates the initial product idea, and defines the project's strategic direction.

The deliverables in this phase form the foundation for all subsequent Business Analysis and Solution Design activities.

---

## Deliverables

- Founder Discovery
- Driver Persona
- Rider Persona
- Driver Journey Map
- Rider Journey Map
- Value Proposition Canvas
- Problem Statement
- Discovery Report

---

## Outcome

At the completion of this phase, the project will have a validated understanding of:

- Business goals
- User needs
- Customer pain points
- Product vision
- Initial business hypotheses

These findings will guide future market research, requirements gathering, and solution design.
